  • 每日听力课：逐字证据、每天最多准备一课，以及诚实的"未知"

    语言： English | 简体中文

    发布日期：2026-11-09

    FLH 原本只处理我输入的问题。听力功能加入了第二个来源：法语视频加上我自己提供的转写稿，生成考试式听力练习；之后每天从播放列表拉取，最多自动准备一课，并可选通过 Telegram 通知。它分两次改动、两天完成。

  • Daily listening lessons: verbatim evidence, one prepared lesson a day, and honest unknowns

    Language: English | 简体中文

    FLH started from questions I typed. The listening work adds a second source: French videos with my own transcripts, turned into exam-style listening exercises, then pulled from a playlist each day with at most one prepared lesson and an optional Telegram notice. It shipped as two changes, over two days.

    My engineering contribution: separating source material from generated exercises, requiring every cited line to be verbatim, capping automatic spending at one attempt per day, and treating a timed-out or lost call as unknown rather than failed.

  • Recall practice from my own questions: hidden answers, reference not grade, nothing stored

    Language: English | 简体中文

    The Knowledge Library could only be browsed. A Concept’s page shows its title, every statement, examples and source links at once, so there was no way to try a question first and check afterwards. Recall practice adds that, starting from a Concept, using only the questions I originally asked.

    My engineering contribution: defining what counts as current support, deciding that the reveal is a reference rather than a grade, and requiring that practice stores nothing and changes nothing.

  • Releasing FLH v1.0: a derived stage summary, a rehearsed rollback and a real-browser smoke

    Language: English | 简体中文

Most of the release work was not new features.   • AI suggestions with human authority: bounded candidates, transactional confirmation and recovery

    Language: English | 简体中文

    I added AI-assisted Concept suggestions to FLH to reduce the manual effort of reviewing extracted knowledge. The model proposes existing Concepts and explains its choice; the user confirms each item before membership changes. The implementation keeps generated evidence, current state and human authority separate.

    My engineering contribution: defining bounded inputs and candidates, designing generation and confirmation as different operations, and hardening their replay, cancellation and stale-state behavior.
